# NeuroEarly

NeuroEarly is an evidence-based neuroscience project exploring the earliest changes associated with neurodegenerative diseases, with a focus on understanding disease mechanisms, early warning patterns, risk factors, and evidence-based risk reduction.

The project is designed as an educational and research tool. It is not intended to diagnose, predict, or replace professional medical assessment.

## Project Goals

- Explain how selected neurodegenerative diseases affect the brain.
- Compare early and prodromal features across diseases.
- Distinguish between general symptoms and more meaningful neurological patterns.
- Explain known risk factors and protective factors.
- Present evidence-based risk reduction strategies.
- Build interactive tools that make neuroscience easier to understand.

## Initial Diseases

- Alzheimer's disease
- Parkinson's disease
- Lewy body dementia

## Planned Features

- Interactive disease comparison
- Early-sign pattern explorer
- Brain-region visualizations
- Disease mechanism explanations
- Risk-factor and risk-reduction modules
- Scientific references and evidence grading
- Clear limitations and medical disclaimers

## Scientific Approach

Information will be based on reputable medical and scientific sources, including peer-reviewed research and organizations such as:

- National Institute on Aging
- National Institute of Neurological Disorders and Stroke
- World Health Organization
- PubMed-indexed research
- Major neurological and medical journals

## Important Medical Disclaimer

NeuroEarly is an educational project and does not provide medical diagnosis or individualized medical advice.

Symptoms associated with neurodegenerative diseases are often nonspecific and can occur for many different reasons. Anyone concerned about neurological symptoms should consult a qualified healthcare professional.

## Development

Research and development began in August 2026.

The project will be developed iteratively, with research, prototyping, testing, feedback, and revision documented throughout the repository.
